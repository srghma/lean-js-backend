Alongside this, `LakeJs/FromLcnf.lean` and `LakeJs/Compile.lean` were reworked to model instantiated parameters, mutual families and erased fields (a field that is a type, a type family or a proof is dropped, and projection indices are renumbered accordingly), and `LakeJs/TyPretty.lean` was rewritten for the new tree. `BACKEND.md` now documents the type tree, the `Shape` extraction, the `.self` restriction and the removal of `dynamic`, and its "known limits" section was corrected.
